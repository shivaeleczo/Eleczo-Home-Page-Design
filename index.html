Buying electrical products online in India

Eleczo supplies electrical products to trade buyers and households across India — switchgear, control gear, wires and cables, wiring accessories and lighting. Trade customers order against drawings and bills of materials; households usually arrive needing one replacement part and some guidance about which one.

Product listings carry the specification detail that matters for selection: current rating, pole configuration, breaking capacity in kA, trip characteristics and enclosure protection rating. Search accepts manufacturer part numbers directly, so a known reference does not have to be translated into category navigation.

Bulk requirements are handled by quotation rather than list pricing. Send a bill of materials and the response covers availability, lead time and delivery staging.
