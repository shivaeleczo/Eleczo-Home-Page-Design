## RS-001 — Company and platform context
**Triggered by:** Discovery
**Method:** Public web sources, August 2026. Live site returned HTTP 520 to automated
fetching, so no first-hand page audit was possible.

**Findings.** Eleczo is the electrical e-commerce platform of Mohan Exim India Pvt Ltd,
serving both B2B and B2C. Public sources cite 40–50+ brands, ~50k products and 600+ certified
sellers, across switchgear, circuit breakers, wires and cables, sockets, contactors, control
gear, distribution boards and lighting. Named brands include Havells, Siemens, Schneider
Electric, L&T and Socomec. Product listings carry dense technical specification (pole count,
breaking capacity in kA, trip unit type, current rating) — evidence that a significant share
of demand is specification-driven rather than browse-driven. An `/upcoming-home` URL exists
on the domain, suggesting redesign work already partly in flight.

**Confidence.** Medium. Marketing copy, self-reported figures, no internal verification.

**Translated into:** AUD table in charter; DEC-001 context; D-4, D-9.
